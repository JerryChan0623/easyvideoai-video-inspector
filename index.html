A few useful answers.

Does this upload or store my video?

No. The page uses a temporary browser object URL to read the selected local file. It does not send the video to a server or save it in browser storage. Clear the file or close the page when finished. If hosted online, the hosting provider receives normal page requests; the video is not part of them.

Why won't my video open?

The container or codec may not be supported by your browser, or the file may be damaged. Try another browser or export an MP4 with a browser-supported codec. This tool does not transcode files.

Can I upload this video to EasyVideoAI?

EasyVideoAI's current studios use prepared video templates and accept character photos, rather than custom source videos. This inspector is useful for reviewing downloaded results or any other local video.

Is the inspector free? Is AI generation also free?

The inspector is free and works without an account. EasyVideoAI video generation is a separate paid service using credits. Public demonstrations can be watched free of charge.
