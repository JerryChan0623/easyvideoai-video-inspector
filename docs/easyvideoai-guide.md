# EasyVideoAI Guide: Create AI Videos From Your Photos

EasyVideoAI turns character photos into videos using prepared dance, performance and character scenes. You choose the scene, assign a photo to each role, then review the length, framing and credit quote before generating. The template provides the performance; your photos provide the cast.

This EasyVideoAI guide walks through a first video, explains how to choose usable photos and shows what to check in the downloaded MP4. It is published by EasyVideoAI. The separate Video Inspector is a free open-source companion for checking video specifications; creating a personalized video uses credits.

## What to prepare before opening a studio

Choose a template on [EasyVideoAI](https://easyvideoai.com/?utm_source=notion&utm_medium=referral&utm_campaign=beginner_guide), then read its photo labels. Some scenes need one portrait; others need two separate character images. A single group photo does not replace two required upload slots.

For your first attempt, choose a sharp photo with one clearly visible subject. Keep the face and head inside the image, avoid heavy shadows and check that another person is not competing for attention. For a supported pet scene, pick a photo where the face, ears and distinctive markings are readable. Making a blurry image larger will not recover missing detail.

The current photo workflow accepts JPEG, PNG and WebP files up to 10 MB each. Check the studio for its current requirements and use images you have permission to edit. See the [photo selection guide](https://easyvideoai.com/ai-video-photo-guide) for more help.

## Create your first video with EasyVideoAI

### 1. Choose the performance you want to keep

Watch the demonstration before uploading. Look at the number of characters, the camera changes and how far people move across the frame. Choose a scene whose performance already fits your idea.

For a one-photo starting point, open the [Bulin 47 studio](https://easyvideoai.com/bulin-47-ai-video-generator). For a two-character dance that supports person or pet references, use the [Smalltown Boy studio](https://easyvideoai.com/smalltown-boy-ai).

The current studios use prepared video templates. They do not accept your own source video or a free-text request for a completely different scene.

### 2. Assign each photo to a continuing character

Match the image slots to the labels in your chosen studio. With two characters, decide who plays each role before uploading. If you reverse the assignments, use the swap control where available and check both thumbnails again.

Smalltown Boy makes the distinction useful: Image 1 is illustrated by the orange cat that starts in front in the public demonstration. Image 2 is illustrated by the black-and-white cat behind it. The characters change positions later. Each photo is meant to follow its assigned character, rather than whichever character happens to stand in front.

For pet references, do not choose a photo simply because its background resembles the demonstration. The face, coat pattern and other distinguishing details are the useful parts of the reference.

### 3. Choose a length, resolution and frame

Read the choices in the studio. Available lengths depend on the template; Smalltown Boy currently offers 12 or 21 seconds, starting at the beginning of its prepared performance. The current studios offer 480p and 720p output options.

Original framing keeps the source composition. Square and vertical choices crop it, so a raised hand or a second character near an edge may disappear. Watch the full scene before deciding whether 9:16 fits both characters. A different frame is not simply a smaller copy of the same composition.

If you are unsure, begin with the shortest available segment that includes the moment you want and compare the displayed quote before proceeding. Length and resolution affect the credits required.

### 4. Review the quote and confirm once

Check the photo assignments, duration, resolution, frame and credit amount together. Public demonstrations are free to watch; creating a personalized video requires credits. Use the [current pricing page](https://easyvideoai.com/pricing) for purchase options and the studio for the exact quote.

Buying credits and submitting a generation are separate steps. After checkout, return to the studio and review the request before the final Generate confirmation. If the status is uncertain, check My videos before submitting again.

Guest access is tied to the browser. Save an account before changing devices or clearing browser data if you want to retain access across devices. A checkout email alone does not restore guest access.

### 5. Review the finished video with sound

Play the whole result before downloading or sharing. Check each character during turns, close-ups and overlaps. A good opening frame does not prove that the face or pet markings remain consistent throughout the dance.

Listen to the beginning and ending as well. The workflow restores audio from the matching prepared source segment. Check timing alongside visible gestures and mouth movements. Generation time and visual details can vary; a completed file still needs your review.

## Why the public demonstration can look different

A demonstration explains the effect and role assignments. It is a separate public asset, not a personalized preview of the photos you upload. A completed case is another finished example, while the generation uses the template's separate prepared input.

For example, Smalltown Boy's public cat demonstration has its own vertical frame and soundtrack. Your generation follows the selected segment and audio of a separate two-person performance. The demonstration's edges and sound should not be treated as an exact preview of your result. The studio explains these differences before you generate.

## Check the downloaded MP4 with the free Video Inspector

Open the [EasyVideoAI Video Inspector](https://jerrychan0623.github.io/easyvideoai-video-inspector/) and select your downloaded video. It reads dimensions, duration, aspect ratio and file size locally. The selected file is not uploaded.

Use a centered crop preview to see which edges a 9:16, 1:1 or 16:9 version would remove. You can copy a plain-text report or save JSON if an editor needs the specifications. This tool checks other local videos too; it does not require an EasyVideoAI account.

The inspector does not edit or export a crop, verify codec or audio presence, or certify a file for any social platform. Review playback and sound yourself, and make any final crop in your video editor.

## Frequently asked questions

### Is EasyVideoAI free?

Public demonstrations are free to watch, and the separate Video Inspector is free. Personalized video generation uses credits. Review the quote before confirming rather than assuming a free generation is included.

### Can I upload my own dance video?

The current studios use prepared performances and accept character photos. The inspector can read a local video, but that does not add custom source-video support to the generator.

### Will my result look exactly like the demonstration?

No exact match is guaranteed. The demonstration may use different identities, framing or sound. Your photos guide the new characters, and generated details can vary. Check the studio's explanation and review the entire result.

### Are my uploads automatically shown as public examples?

Uploads and generated results are private and are not automatically added to the public examples. Read the [privacy policy](https://easyvideoai.com/privacy) and [terms](https://easyvideoai.com/terms) before creating or sharing a video.
